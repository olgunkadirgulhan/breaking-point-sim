Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The Breaking Point Sim publishing tool is used only by the channel owner to upload original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • The tool requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos, manage the channel's playlists and branding, read video statistics, and post or reply to comments on the owner's own videos.
  • OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never shared or sold.
  • The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or of any other users.
  • Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
